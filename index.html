💭 How It Started

Velavah Goli Soda is born from a love for authentic, traditional Indian beverages. Our carbonated goli sodas are crafted to deliver the boldest fizz and most vibrant fruit flavors — bringing the iconic Codd-neck bottle experience to every generation across Tamil Nadu!

🥤 What Makes Us Special

Every Codd-neck bottle is a labor of love - filled with high-carbonation CO₂-pressurized soda and sealed with the classic glass marble that pops open with a satisfying burst. Bold flavors, powerful fizz, and the thrill of every pop!

What is Velavah Goli Soda made of?

+

Velavah Goli Soda is made with carbonated water, fruit flavors, sugar, citric acid, and food-grade CO₂ for that signature fizzy kick. Each bottle is sealed under pressure using the classic Codd-neck marble mechanism — delivering consistent, bold flavor in every sip! 🫧

🍋
02

What flavors does Velavah Goli Soda come in?

+

We offer 6 unique handcrafted flavors: Lemon Fizz 🍋 (our bestseller!), Blueberry Blast 🫐 (new!), Pineapple Burst 🍍, Rose Bliss 🌹, Ginger Snap 🫚, and Grape Splash 🍇. Each bursts with bold fruit flavors for a vibrant, refreshing taste!

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
03

Is Velavah Goli Soda safe for children?

+

Absolutely! Velavah Goli Soda is 100% non-alcoholic and family-friendly. Our carbonated goli sodas are loved by children, adults, and grandparents alike. The fizzy CO₂ kick is all part of the fun — that's exactly why our tagline is "Made for Every Generation." ✨

05

What makes goli soda different from regular soda?

+

Goli soda (also known as marble soda or Codd-neck bottle soda) is a traditional Indian carbonated drink sealed with a glass marble. The marble is pushed in to release CO₂ pressure and unlock the fizz! 💥 Velavah takes this beloved heritage and elevates it with bold fruit flavors, powerful carbonation, and vibrant fizzy taste.
