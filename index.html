Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Velavah Goli Soda.

What is Velavah Goli Soda made of?

Velavah Goli Soda is made with carbonated water, artificial fruit flavors, sugar, citric acid, and food-grade CO₂ for that signature fizzy kick. Each bottle is sealed under pressure using the classic Codd-neck marble mechanism — delivering consistent, bold flavor in every sip.

What flavors does Velavah Goli Soda come in?

We offer 6 unique handcrafted flavors: Lemon Fizz (our bestseller), Cool Mojito (new!), Pineapple Burst, Rose Bliss, Ginger Snap, and Grape Splash. Each is made with artificial fruit flavors for a vibrant taste.

Is Velavah Goli Soda safe for children?

Absolutely! Velavah Goli Soda is 100% non-alcoholic and family-friendly. Our carbonated sodas are enjoyed by children, adults, and the elderly alike. Just be mindful of the CO₂ fizz — that's part of the fun! That's why our tagline is "Made for Every Generation."

Where can I buy Velavah Goli Soda in Namakkal?

Velavah Goli Soda is available in select retail stores across Namakkal, Tiruchengode, Salem, Erode, Karur, Trichy, Coimbatore, and all of Tamil Nadu. Contact us at +91 96000 10922 or via WhatsApp to place bulk orders or find the nearest retailer.

What makes goli soda different from regular soda?

Goli soda (also known as marble soda or Codd-neck bottle soda) is a traditional Indian carbonated drink sealed with a glass marble. The marble is pushed in to release CO₂ pressure and unlock the fizz. Velavah takes this beloved heritage and elevates it with bold artificial flavors, powerful carbonation, and vibrant fizzy taste.
